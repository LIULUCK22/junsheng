I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Washington University in St. Louis. My research interests include coding theory, error-correcting codes, nonlinear coding, combinatorics, and computational approaches to mathematical research.

My current research with Prof. Netanel Raviv studies redundancy and rate of 2D and 3D nonlinear coding, as well as applications of error-correcting codes in two- and three-dimensional settings.
